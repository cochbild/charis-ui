# Code of conduct

This code of conduct is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in rust-ui to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive
and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind and patient with other people, especially newcomers.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving constructive feedback, and accepting it gracefully.
- Taking responsibility for our mistakes, apologizing to those affected, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project, not only for ourselves.

Examples of behavior that is not acceptable:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and any other channel run by the project. It also applies when someone officially
represents the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this
code of conduct, and they will say why when that's appropriate.

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers privately, through the contact listed on the
repository owner's GitHub profile. Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and
fairly, and the maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person reporting.

## Enforcement guidelines

The maintainers follow these guidelines when deciding on the consequences of an action:

1. **Correction.** For inappropriate language or other behavior deemed unprofessional or
   unwelcome: a private, written warning that explains the violation, and possibly a request for
   a public apology.
2. **Warning.** For a single violation or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for
   continued behavior. The person must not interact with the people involved for a set time,
   including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing the code of conduct.
3. **Temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behavior: a
   temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of violations, harassment of an individual, or aggression
   toward or disparagement of groups of people: a permanent ban from any public interaction
   within the community.
